## 2. 异常检测查询 - 识别异常延迟模式

pipeline:*
| where processing_time_ms > 0
| stats avg(processing_time_ms) as avg_latency,
       stddev(processing_time_ms) as stddev_latency,
       percentiles(processing_time_ms, 95) as p95_latency
       by pipeline, stage, kubernetes.pod_name
| eval threshold = avg_latency + (3 * stddev_latency)
| where p95_latency > threshold

pipeline:* AND trace_id:*
| where @timestamp > now()-1h
| stats count() as event_count,
       min(@timestamp) as start_time,
       max(@timestamp) as end_time,
       values(event_type) as event_types
       by trace_id, pipeline
| eval duration_ms = (end_time - start_time) / 1000000
| sort duration_ms desc

## 6. 按时间段的错误率趋势 - 高级时间分析

level:ERROR
| timechart span=15m
       count() as error_count,
